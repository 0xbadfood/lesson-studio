Purpose

So that no beat falls back to a blank backdrop, physics films need these verbs and extensions. We take them up one verb at a time, alongside other work.

The list comes from three places:

  • The 43 beats where the planner wrote "none fits" while refilling blank scenes in published films.
  • Reading the contact sheets for every Class 10–12 physics film.
  • The ICSE Selina Class 10 physics book, checked against the CISCE syllabus topics.

Order and acceptance

Build kirchhoff first: it alone accounts for four blank beats in one film. Then build the three ICSE verbs, then the extensions grouped by verb file, so each file is touched once.

  1. The four planner routing fixes. They are the cheapest, and they need no Linkwork change.
  2. kirchhoff, then pulley, sound and em_device.
  3. Extensions with two or more waiting beats: diffraction, em_waves, current, gas_process, heat_engine, polaroid.
  4. The single-beat extensions and the open nits.
  5. vertical_circle and collision_2d.

A verb or show is accepted when:

  • It enters as trial, so films can use it but it isn't trusted yet.
  • Its test checks the book's own numbers: the worked example the beat quotes, computed by the verb and not typed in.
  • Its example renders legibly at full and half frame, which the size test enforces.
  • A rebeat of the beats listed here fills them with the verb, and the contact sheet reads right against the narration.

This list is also the first worklist for the gated verb factory, once we plan it.

Progress

Batch 1, circuits (4 November 2026): done. 9 of the 17 blank beats in seven films are now drawn, and those films are re-published.

  • New verb kirchhoff: solves any network, with Example 3.6's 5/8, 5/2 and 15/8 A and CD at zero. It refuses a wire drawn through a junction.
  • New current options: join (two slabs, with result: false for the set-up only) and drift with field: false.
  • Routing fixes in the ladder and rebeat: they offer the verbs of the beats either side and graph for a trend, and they match on the narration. The reviewer now counts values the film has already stated.
  • Filled: leph1-03 C9 S02, S16, S31, S32; C2 S07, S26; leph1-04 C8 S32; keph2-05 C9 S19; keph1-05 C4 S17.
  • Left, rightly: leph1-03 C9 S24 names Fig. 3.17's parts before the figure shows their wiring. leph1-04 C8 S30 is about quantum standards, and S38 says only "1 C is 1 A s".
  • Still blank, for later batches: leph1-01 C3 S03 and S31 (three resultants tip to tail); keph1-05 C4 S21 (the work verb's "N and mg do no work" is false for a falling body); keph2-01 C2 S20 and S21 (the elastic hydraulic view says too much).
